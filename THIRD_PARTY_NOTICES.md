## Bundled material

- The [airline lookup table](lib/airline-codes.ts) is derived from the OpenFlights Airline Database. The table selects airlines with both IATA and ICAO codes, favors active operators, and includes corrections for reassigned codes. The derived database is available under ODbL 1.0 and rights in individual contents are licensed under DbCL 1.0. [Source](https://openflights.org/data.php#license) · [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) · [DbCL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/)

## External services

- **OpenSky Network** provides global aircraft positions and observed tracks. Review its data license and written-permission requirements before operational or commercial use. [OpenSky terms](https://opensky-network.org/about/terms-of-use)
- **adsb.lol** and **ADSB.fi** provide regional aircraft positions. **adsbdb** supplies aircraft and route metadata. [adsb.lol](https://www.adsb.lol/) · [ADSB.fi](https://adsb.fi/) · [adsbdb](https://www.adsbdb.com/)
- **Planespotters.net** serves aircraft photos. Skytrace credits the photographer and links to the original photo when one is available. [Photo API](https://www.planespotters.net/photo/api)
- **Esri World Imagery** supplies satellite imagery, with contributions from Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community. Its terms apply to the imagery. [Layer details and terms](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9)
- **Mapzen Terrarium** supplies elevation tiles derived from several providers. See its attribution guidance for the source datasets. [Terrain attribution](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md)
- **OpenFreeMap** serves vector tiles for buildings, bridges, and land cover. © OpenMapTiles. Data from OpenStreetMap contributors under the ODbL. [OpenFreeMap attribution](https://openfreemap.org/#attribution) · [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
